# Skill-Setu
AI-powered vocational training in regional languages.

# Project Vision

Skill-Setu is an AI-powered, vernacular mobile learning platform designed to bridge the divide between rural youth aspirations and modern economic opportunities. By delivering practical vocational training in native Indian languages through gamified simulations and adaptive AI tutoring, the platform converts untapped human potential into job-ready talent for high-growth industries.The Core Challenge

# The Language Wall: 

High-quality vocational and technical training in India is predominantly taught in English, effectively locking millions of ambitious non-English speakers out of the modern economy.

Passive Learning Gap: Traditional video-based learning lacks practical engagement, leading to low completion rates and insufficient hands-on capability for physical technical roles.

# Key Features & System WorkflowKey Features & System Workflow:

Personalized Vernacular Onboarding: An AI conversational agent interacts with users in their regional language to evaluate background skills, career goals, and personal interests, building a tailored learning path.

Gamified Practical Simulations: Replaces static videos with interactive, touch-based simulations (e.g., virtually wiring a circuit or assembling a solar panel) to build muscle memory and practical mastery.

Adaptive AI Tutor: Continuously tracks user performance, dynamically adjusting module speed and providing supplemental regional-language explanations when a student encounters difficult topics.

Naukri Connect & Verified Certification: Upon demonstrating mastery, users earn digital certificates and gain direct visibility on an integrated hiring portal that connects them with corporate employers.

# Socio-Economic Impact:

Bridging the Bharat Divide: Connects urban and rural economic ecosystems by granting non-English speakers equal access to high-paying technical careers.

Industry Readiness: Directly targets critical workforce shortages in priority sectors like green energy, electric mobility, and advanced manufacturing.

Scalable Empowerment: Provides a clear, dignified path from skill acquisition to sustainable income generation.
